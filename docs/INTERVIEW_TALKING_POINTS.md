# Interview Talking Points

1. Start with the decision: recurring market updates should connect price, raw materials, output, demand and margins—not list them independently.
2. Explain the cost bridge: BF-BOF and EAF routes create different ore/coal/scrap exposure; the blend is transparent and editable.
3. Walk through the model: demand and supply assumptions determine tightness; elasticity links tightness to price; cost inflation and EAF transition shape cash cost.
4. Show analytical caution: scenarios are decision tools, not forecasts; public tonnage is separated from synthetic modeled economics.
5. Demonstrate execution: SQL, workbook, dashboard, deck and BI-ready exports reconcile to the same deterministic data.

## Truthful resume bullet options
- Built an independent China steel strategy portfolio using deterministic synthetic market data and cited public sources, integrating weekly/monthly updates, raw-material cost and margin bridges, 2025–2030 scenarios, competitor screening, SQL, Excel and an interactive dashboard.
- Translated China steel policy and public producer data into an auditable strategy case with visible assumptions, sensitivity analysis, quality checks and an editable executive deck; all findings are explicitly labeled as portfolio-model outputs.
